Colombian-style, meat & potato

Ingredients

Dough:

  • 2 cups masarepa (yellow, precooked)
  • Warm water
  • Salt
  • Splash of oil

Filling:

  • Ground beef or shredded beef
  • Boiled/mashed potatoes
  • Onion
  • Scallions
  • Garlic
  • Cumin
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Make the dough: mix masarepa, warm water, salt, and a splash of oil, kneading until soft and pliable. Cover and rest 10–15 minutes.
  2. Make the filling: sauté chopped onion, scallions, and garlic until fragrant, then add ground beef, mashed potatoes, cumin, salt, and pepper, cooking until well combined. Let cool.
  3. Flatten small balls of dough into thin circles (between plastic sheets works well), fill with a spoonful of filling, and fold closed, sealing the edges.
  4. Deep fry until golden brown and crispy.
  5. Serve with ají (Colombian hot sauce) — made from chopped scallions, cilantro, tomato, and hot sauce.
